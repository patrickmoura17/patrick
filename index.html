About us

One team for every surface

Most kitchen and bath remodels stall in the gaps between trades. The countertop company waits on the tile setter, the tile setter waits on the floor, and you end up managing all three.

Surffaces handles countertops, tile and flooring together. We help you choose materials that work with each other, measure the whole room once, and schedule the work in the right order. You get one point of contact, one quote and one crew responsible for the finish.

FAQ

Questions we hear most

How long does a countertop installation take?

Once your cabinets are installed and level, we template the counters. Fabrication usually takes one to two weeks after templating, and most kitchens are installed in a single day. Your sink and cooktop can usually be reconnected the next day.

Quartz, granite or quartzite: which should I choose?

Quartz is non-porous and never needs sealing, but it doesn't like direct heat. Granite and quartzite are natural stone, handle heat well and need occasional sealing. Quartzite gives you a marble look with far more durability. Our countertop guide compares all three.

What's the best tile for a bathroom floor or shower?

Porcelain, in most cases. It absorbs almost no water and holds up to daily use. For floors, choose a tile rated for wet areas. Smaller tiles add more grout lines, which give better grip in showers.

What flooring works best in a kitchen?

Luxury vinyl plank and porcelain tile handle spills best. Engineered hardwood gives you real wood that copes better with humidity than solid boards. Our flooring guide walks through the trade-offs.

Do you install, or only sell materials?

Both. Most clients have us supply and install so one team is responsible for the result. If you have your own installer, we can supply materials only.

How does pricing work?

After an on-site measure, you get a written quote that lists materials, preparation, installation and cleanup separately. Prices depend on the material, square footage, edge profiles and how much prep the room needs.

Do you work with contractors and designers?

Yes. We supply and install for remodelers, builders, designers and property managers. Contact us about trade accounts.
