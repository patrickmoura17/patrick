Our process

From first sketch to final fit

  1. 01 /

    Consultation

    We visit, measure and listen — how you use the room, what you like, and your budget.

  2. 02 /

    Design

    Drawings and 3D renderings so you can see the layout before anything is built.

  3. 03 /

    Materials

    Choose woods, finishes and hardware from samples you can hold.

  4. 04 /

    Fabrication

    Everything is cut and assembled in our shop to exact tolerances.

  5. 05 /

    Installation

    Our own crew installs, levels and protects your home along the way.

  6. 06 /

    Walkthrough

    We review every door and drawer with you and fix anything before we call it done.
