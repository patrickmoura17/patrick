What we offer

Built from the ground up

Planning a remodel or replacing one room? Start with the surface you need.

Porcelain, ceramic, natural stone and mosaics for floors, walls, backsplashes and showers.

Hardwood, engineered wood, luxury vinyl plank and laminate — for a single room or the whole house.

Quartz, granite, marble and quartzite for kitchens, islands, vanities and bars — templated and cut to fit.

Professional installation for everything we sell, from subfloor prep and waterproofing to the final grout line.

Our process

From sample to finished surface

  1. 01 /

    Consultation

    Tell us about the space, how it's used, your style and your budget.

  2. 02 /

    Selection

    Compare samples side by side and narrow down the tile, flooring or stone that fits.

  3. 03 /

    Measure

    We measure on site so quantities, layouts and seams are planned before anything is ordered.

  4. 04 /

    Quote

    A clear written quote covering materials, preparation and installation.

  5. 05 /

    Installation

    Surfaces are prepared, laid and finished with care, and your home is protected along the way.

  6. 06 /

    Walkthrough

    We check every joint, edge and seam with you before we call the job done.
